How it works

Everything stays inside your school, and sessions happen at school or in a supervised online room.

  1. Make a profileLearners list their class and subjects. Mentors add their areas of strength, topics and free time.
  2. Get verifiedMentors submit school documents for verification, recommendation before they show a verified badge.
  3. Book a sessionChoose a verified mentor and request an open 30-minute slot. The mentor confirms the booking.
  4. Meet and rateYour booked chat is available during the 30-minute session. Session tools stay available from your Sessions tab.

Simple and free to start

Learners and mentors both join for free, and the green Verified badge is always free. Learners can request 30-minute sessions with verified mentors.
