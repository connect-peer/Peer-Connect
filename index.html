How it works

Everything stays inside your school, and sessions happen at school or in a supervised online room.

  1. Make a profileStudents list their grade and subjects. Tutors add marks, topics and free time.
  2. Get verifiedTutors pass a short topic test or get a teacher’s recommendation before they show a verified badge.
  3. ConnectSend a request with the topic you need help with. The tutor accepts and picks a time.
  4. Meet and rateAfter a 45-minute session, both sides leave short feedback that builds the tutor’s profile.

Simple and free to start

Students and tutors both join for free, and the green Verified badge is always free. Every student can send unlimited connection requests.
